Arranger ST

An arrange-window sequencer with eight DS-10 style synths, a mixer with inserts and two send buses, and a KAOSS pad for recording knob moves. Click once to start the audio engine, then press Space. Letter keys play notes. Your song saves itself in this browser.

Sound and automation

Synth windowDS-10 layout: VCO, VCO1, VCO2, VCF and EG columns, the patch row underneath, plus a 4-pole highpass and voice count per track.
Knobs on screenDrag vertically, scroll, or double-click to reset. Shift for fine steps.
Patch rowEach destination has a depth knob and a source box: SIN, SAW, SQR, S&H (the MG), EG or VCO2.
Mixer slotsClick a slot to pick DS-10 FX, Midiverb, Tape delay or Distortion, bypass it or open its editor.
Recording automationPress Record and play, then move the KAOSS pad or any knob of the active track. Each move lands in the part under the playhead.
Automation lanesIn Key Edit, click the lane name under the grid to switch between velocity and the part's knob lanes; scroll it to add any knob. Draw with the pointer or pencil.
SavingChanges save to this browser as you work. File ▸ Save JSON writes a file; File ▸ Open JSON loads one.

About the sound

  • The voice is a port of the ds10emu reimplementation of the DS-10. Knob ranges, the LFO, envelope, portamento, filter mapping and VCA run at the DS-10's 512 Hz control rate, as on the Nintendo DS.
  • Oscillators are polyBLEP, and the filter is a TPT state-variable filter with clipped states, instead of the DS-10's aliasing oscillators and Chamberlin filter.
  • Delay, chorus and flanger use the DS-10's single delay-line algorithm with its three parameter mappings.
  • Midiverb, Midifex and Midiverb II programs are converted from the midiverb_emulator decompilation and run at 24 kHz. The tape delay is a port of camouflage FakeTapeDelay.

Each track is mono (legato, with portamento) or polyphonic up to 8 voices; set it in the Synth window.
